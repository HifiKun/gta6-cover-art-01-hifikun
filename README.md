Vice Cover Maker — a GTA 6-style cover art maker. Load up to three of your own photos, crop and position each one, fill a multi-panel poster (trio, duo or single hero), pick a Vice City sunset, neon night or pastel grade, write a bold caption, and export a PNG at 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 or 1920 × 1080. Everything runs offline in the browser: photos are read on your own machine and never uploaded. Original artwork — no Rockstar logo, artwork or font; the display type is Anton (SIL Open Font License), shipped under app/fonts/.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080, load a photo (or try a sample), and hit Export PNG.
